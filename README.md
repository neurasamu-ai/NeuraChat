# NeuraChat
NeuraChat — Communication, Elevated.

One app. Every conversation. Limitless intelligence.

Meet NeuraChat, a new standard for mobile communication. Bringing messaging, native calling, and powerful AI into one sophisticated experience, NeuraChat combines premium design with the freedom, flexibility, and control modern users deserve.

Built for those who expect more from their technology, NeuraChat transforms everyday communication into something smarter, smoother, and more personal.

MESSAGING, REIMAGINED

Experience SMS with a modern interface designed around simplicity, personalization, and control.

• Beautiful threaded conversations with timestamps and dates • Custom message bubble colors • Personalized chat backgrounds with zoom, pan, and opacity controls • Contact names and avatars directly inside conversations • Multi-SIM support with clear SIM identification • One-tap retry for failed messages • Scheduled SMS with delivery status • MMS support for pictures and rich media

Every conversation feels personal. Every detail feels intentional.

CALLING, REDEFINED

Enjoy a refined native calling experience designed to make staying connected feel effortless.

• Full-screen incoming call interface • Caller names, numbers, and contact avatars • Convenient answer and decline controls • In-call mute, speaker, hold, and DTMF keypad • Lock-screen calling support • Call waiting and call-blocking options • Default dialer support for an integrated phone experience

A cleaner interface. Greater control. Communication without unnecessary complexity.

YOUR AI. YOUR MODELS. YOUR RULES.

Discover a world of artificial intelligence through one unified interface.

NeuraChat brings multiple AI ecosystems together, giving you the flexibility to choose your preferred providers, explore different models, and connect your own AI infrastructure.

Supported providers and ecosystems include:

• OpenAI — ChatGPT models • Google — Gemini • Anthropic — Claude • DeepSeek • xAI — Grok • Alibaba — Qwen • OpenRouter • Z.AI • Custom and self-hosted AI endpoints

Connect compatible services such as LM Studio, Ollama, llama.cpp, or your own OpenAI-compatible server.

Bring your own API key, select your preferred model, and build an AI experience around your needs. Switch providers without being locked into a single ecosystem.

Your AI experience. Your infrastructure. Your choice.

Provider and model availability depends on your configuration, credentials, and the services you use.
